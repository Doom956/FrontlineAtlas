How to play Frontline Atlas

Frontline Atlas is a free strategy game that runs in your browser. You start as one small territory on a map and grow it until you own most of the land. This guide covers the first minutes.

The goal

You win by owning a set share of all land (75 percent by default, and you can set it between 60 and 90 percent in the setup screen), or by being the last nation standing. If a game runs long, the share you need slowly drops, so games finish. You lose when your territory is gone.

Your first minutes

  • Pick your starting spot on the map.
  • Attack neutral land first. Choose a target and every border you share with it advances at once. You choose how big a share of your troops goes into the attack.
  • Build cities, mines and oil wells to earn gold, iron and oil. Buildings can be upgraded to higher levels.
  • Keep an eye on your troops. They grow over time, and one pool is shared by your attacks and your defense.

Research and armies

Research opens new options. There are four pillars to choose from: Navy, Air, Armor and Defense. You commit to a path in each pillar and can master at most two of them in a single game, so every game plays differently. Tanks, warships, bombers, cruise missiles and atom bombs are all on the menu, and the Defense pillar adds Flak, Tracking Radar, Tank Traps and Coastal Batteries.

Other nations

AI nations and tribes share the map with you. You can offer alliances, sign peace deals and ask for non-aggression pacts. Break an alliance and you are marked as a traitor for a short time, so think before you do it.

Controls

Left click attacks or selects. Drag the map to move around and use the mouse wheel to zoom. On a phone or tablet you use touch, and long-press opens the radial menu. Every main button has an icon and a plain word, and the game has a help screen.

Ready? The best way to learn is to play one short game against bots.
