Features of Frontline Atlas

Frontline Atlas is a free browser strategy game about territory control. Here is what you will find in it.

Whole fronts, one click

Pick who you attack and every border you share with that nation moves at once. The longer the border, the faster the front.

Four pillars with masteries

Research is split into Navy, Air, Armor and Defense. You commit to a path in each pillar, and you can reach mastery in at most two pillars per game. The Defense pillar has Flak, Tracking Radar, Tank Traps, Coastal Batteries and Counteroffensive.

Strikes and missiles

Use cruise missiles, stealth bombers, anti-radar missiles and atom bombs. Strikes aim with help from the game, and you can fire salvos.

Diplomacy

Form alliances, sign peace deals where each side pays a term in gold or land, and agree to non-aggression pacts. AI nations decide from how much they like and fear you.

Maps

There are 27 maps: real world maps, Europe 1939, the Roman Empire and a section of fantasy worlds, plus random maps in four sizes.

Art and sound

Every unit and building is drawn in detailed pixel art, and upgrades show on the model. The game has real recorded sounds and music.

Play your way

  • Single player against AI nations on four difficulties.
  • Online free for all, team and special lobbies, plus private lobbies and spectators.
  • Works on phones and tablets as well as desktop.
  • Free, with no download and no account.
