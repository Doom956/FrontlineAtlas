Game modes and multiplayer

You can play Frontline Atlas alone or with other people online. Both use the same rules and the same maps.

Single player

Play against AI nations and neutral tribes. Choose the map, the number of nations and tribes, and a difficulty: easy, normal, hard or brutal. You can pause and change the game speed, and you can keep playing after a game ends if you want to.

Free for all and teams

In free for all, every nation plays for itself. In teams mode you can set from 2 to 6 teams. Teammates are allied and share a colour family, and a team wins together by adding up its land.

Game options

  • Victory land share from 60 to 90 percent, and an optional time limit.
  • Random spawn, a compact map, and a peace period of 2, 4 or 6 minutes at the start.
  • More starting gold, faster building, faster growth, or a fast game.
  • Fog of war.
  • Switch off nukes, air, navy, SAM sites, alliances or research.

Multiplayer lobbies

  • Public games: three kinds are always open: free for all, teams and special, which adds one to three random modifiers. AI nations fill empty places, and the map changes from game to game.
  • Private lobbies: the full setup screen plus a seat count. Share the code or an invite link with friends.
  • Spectators: watch a running game with the whole map in view. A player who has fallen keeps watching.
  • Reconnect: if your connection drops, you rejoin and catch up by yourself.

Only the host of a private game can pause it. Public games never pause. The game works on desktop, tablet and phone, with no download and no account.
