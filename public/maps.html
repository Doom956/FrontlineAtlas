Maps in Frontline Atlas

Frontline Atlas has 28 hand-built maps and random maps you can generate. Every map shows real ground: fields, forests, hills, mountains and snow. Real maps place AI nations on their real capitals and use real country names.

Real world maps

  • World: the whole Earth.
  • Continents: Europe, Asia, Africa, North America, South America and Oceania.
  • Regions: Middle East, Mediterranean, British Isles, East Asia, Scandinavia, India, Balkans, Central America, Baltic, Japan, Caribbean, Indonesia and Pacific Islands.

History maps

  • Europe 1939: Europe on the eve of the war. Nations start from their 1939 capitals such as Berlin, Paris, London, Rome and Moscow.
  • Roman Empire: the ancient Mediterranean, from Britannia to Syria and the Black Sea.

Fantasy worlds

  • The Ringlands: a walled country with three round walls.
  • The Sundered Sea: a walled island across a wide channel from a vast mainland.
  • The Sundering Wall: one towering continent wall that cuts the world into four seas.
  • The Drifting Isles: floating isles where fleets and aircraft rule.
  • The Fivefold Lands: five great countries with small buffer states between them.
  • The Dark Beyond: six known continents in a lake, ringed by a vast unsettled dark continent.

Random maps

Generate a map as continents, pangaea, archipelago or highlands, in small, medium, large or huge sizes. A compact option halves the map for faster games.

Each map has a suggested number of nations and tribes, and you can change both before you start. Pick a small map for a quick game or the world map for a long campaign.
