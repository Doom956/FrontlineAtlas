# Credits

Frontline Atlas is made by Doom956. The art is drawn in code. The sounds and music are recordings and music made by
people, trimmed, levelled and re-encoded for the game (`public/audio/`). The game also lists these in its Credits
section (title screen, and Menu in a game).
