Frontline Atlas: free browser strategy game

Frontline Atlas is a free strategy game that runs in your browser, with no download and no account. Start as one small territory, push your borders across the map, build an economy, research navies, air power, armor and defenses, and take most of the land. Play alone against AI nations or online in free for all, team and special lobbies.

Choose from 28 maps, from the whole world and real regions to Europe 1939, the Roman Empire and fantasy worlds. It works on desktop, tablet and phone.
